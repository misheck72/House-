01 / THE AURELIA APPROACH

Education should expand a young person's sense of what is possible.

Aurelia is an independent learning community built around intellectual curiosity, character and purposeful action. From the first question in a laboratory to the final rehearsal before opening night, students are given space to think deeply and act with purpose.

02 / A DIFFERENT KIND OF SCHOOL

Rigour with room to wonder.

We combine ambitious academics with an education that values questions as much as answers. Students are challenged to think, make, test, collaborate and learn from people unlike themselves.
